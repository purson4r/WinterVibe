WinterVibe v0.1.0 Beta — Windows x64

1. Extract this ZIP to a folder.
2. Run WinterVibe.exe. The app appears in the notification area (system tray).
3. Open its tray menu to choose a snowfall profile or adjust custom settings.
4. Choose Quit / Выход in the tray menu to close the app.

The app is portable and does not require administrator privileges.
